FAQ

Before
you book

The questions clients ask on almost every first call.

How much does a website cost?

Fixes start from $25 and a business website starts from $120 on my Upwork catalog. Larger builds, stores and redesigns get a fixed quote after a short call, so you know the full price before any work starts.

How long does it take?

Most business websites take 1 to 4 weeks. You get a homepage draft within 2 to 3 days of starting. Fixes are usually done within a day or two.

Can you build from my Figma design or a reference site?

Yes. Most of my builds start from a Figma file, an AI-generated design or a site you like. I rebuild it pixel-accurate in Elementor Pro with global styles, so your team can edit it later.

How many revisions do I get?

Revisions within the agreed scope are included. Small changes along the way, like swapping images or adjusting sections, are part of the job.

WordPress, Elementor or something else?

For most businesses, WordPress with Elementor Pro is the right call: fast to build, easy to edit and good for SEO. If speed is the whole brief, I also build with Next.js or Astro.

Do I own the website?

Yes, 100%. Everything is built on your hosting and accounts. You get full admin access and a Loom walkthrough of how to edit it.

What happens after launch?

I stay on the project. You get a plan for what to improve next, and I offer monthly maintenance: updates, backups, security and small edits.

How do we communicate?

Directly with me, through Upwork messages, email or Zoom. I share progress with short Loom videos and reply within a few hours.
